The charter · rule 1

Every statement about what has happened or what exists is drawn from a cited source and listed in APA references at the end of the post. Every statement about what may happen sits in a passage marked *Speculation*, or in a Signal's forecast. Where the record is uncertain or disputed, the post says so.
