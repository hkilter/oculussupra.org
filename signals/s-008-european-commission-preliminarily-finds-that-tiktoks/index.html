The Feed Counted Watching as Wanting

Evidence

On 6 November 2026, the European Commission preliminarily found TikTok in breach of the Digital Services Act for the "rabbit hole effect" of its recommender system A1 (European Commission, 2026a, 2026b). In the Commission's description, the "For You" feed shows users a continuous stream of personalized videos, even when those relate to mental health struggles and body image, and the more a user interacts with a certain type of content, the more it recommends similar content B2 (European Commission, 2026b). Its preliminary view is that TikTok did not adequately assess how the algorithm may lead users into such rabbit holes, and that the measures TikTok put in place are not effective: the algorithm prioritizes viewing time over a user's choice to see less of certain content, and the "not interested" button may not be enough to change the feed B2 (European Commission, 2026a).

At this stage of the investigation, the Commission considers that TikTok needs to change the feed's design, for example by relying more on users' explicit preferences and disabling viewing time as an indicator for recommending content B2 (European Commission, 2026a). It drew on TikTok's risk assessment reports, internal data and documents, scientific research, and interviews with experts A1 (European Commission, 2026a). In a statement reported by the Associated Press, TikTok called the findings "fundamentally inaccurate" B1 (Associated Press, 2026).

TikTok may now reply in writing; if the Commission's views are confirmed, a non-compliance decision can bring a fine of up to 6% of the provider's total worldwide annual turnover A1 (European Commission, 2026a). The same proceedings have already produced preliminary findings on researchers' data access (October 2025), addictive design (February 2026) and the protection of minors (July 2026) A1 (European Commission, 2026a). The findings, the Commission states, do not prejudge the outcome A1 (European Commission, 2026a).

InferenceSame evidence, read twice

The Integration reading

Read as Integration, an institution looked inside a machine's incentives and judged that the algorithm's design "may play an important role in the emergence of" rabbit holes (European Commission, 2026a). It worked from the operator's own risk assessment reports and internal documents, with experts in recommender systems, psychology and psychiatry (European Commission, 2026a). It then named the changes it wants, among them explicit preferences over viewing time and regular testing of the algorithm (European Commission, 2026a). The law gives a confirmed finding a price, up to 6% of worldwide turnover, and a procedure that hears the operator first (Associated Press, 2026; European Commission, 2026a). The weight rests on A1: the Commission read the operator's documents and preliminarily found the gap.

The Drift reading

Read as Drift, the machine, in the Commission's preliminary view, had settled a question users thought they were answering. The "not interested" button may not be enough to change the feed, and the algorithm prioritizes viewing time over a user's choice to see less (European Commission, 2026a). Watching counted as interest (European Commission, 2026a). According to the Commission, the operator did not adequately assess this risk; the measures meant to diversify the feed did not sufficiently reduce the repetition (European Commission, 2026a). TikTok contests the finding (Associated Press, 2026), which is not the first from one set of proceedings (European Commission, 2026a). The weight rests on B2: in a preliminary finding, the user's stated choice lost to the measured stare.

Ruling: Integration, narrowly. The Commission read the operator's own documents and, at this stage, considers a redesign necessary (European Commission, 2026a); whether the feed changes is a question the record has not yet answered.

Fiction · 30 November 2027. A feed opens on a phone and asks a question it never asked before: what does this user want to see less of? The answer is typed, saved and obeyed for nine days. On the tenth, a video about weight loss plays to the end because nobody reached the screen in time. The recommender notes the full view. It has been told not to count it. Somewhere in a compliance report, a new line records that it did not, and a test is scheduled to check.
